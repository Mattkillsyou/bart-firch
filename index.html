About

I’ve spent nearly twenty years studying the human body, testing its limits, and teaching people how to reclaim theirs.

I’m an athlete, an educator, and a student of a machine that never stops teaching me.

I don’t sell workouts. I teach people how to become stronger, more capable, and harder to break.

Your body is your responsibility. Helping you master it is mine.
